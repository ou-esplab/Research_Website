Our Research

About the Lab

The Earth System Prediction (ESP) Lab, led by Dr. Kathy Pegion at the University of Oklahoma, focuses on understanding and improving predictions of Earth system variability on subseasonal to seasonal (S2S) timescales — from two weeks to a season ahead.

Our work advances the science behind extended-range weather and climate forecasts, with the goal of delivering more reliable predictions for decision-makers in agriculture, water resources, energy, and public health.

Multi-Model Ensembles

Leveraging the North American Multi-Model Ensemble (NMME) and other coordinated prediction systems to characterize forecast uncertainty.

Earth System Processes

Studying coupled atmosphere–ocean–land interactions that drive predictability on extended timescales.
